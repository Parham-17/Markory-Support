Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026
Markory is designed to be private by default.

What we collect

Nothing. Markory does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Your data stays on your device

Memories — the titles, dates, notes, and posters you create are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). They are never uploaded to a server.

Location — used only while you are actively using the app, to help you choose and browse places on the map. Your location is never stored remotely or shared.

Photos — any photo you attach is chosen by you and stored locally. Photos are never uploaded.

Sharing

A poster or image leaves your device only when you explicitly tap Share and choose where to send it. That action is entirely yours.

Children

Markory is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.
