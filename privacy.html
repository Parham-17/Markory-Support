Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
Markory is designed to be private by default.

What we collect

Nothing through the app. Markory collects no personal data and sends us nothing, and we never sell personal data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you send, and we use them only to reply to you and to fix what you report. If you write an App Store review, we can read it and may reply to it on the App Store. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which we use only to fix problems and improve Markory, and you can turn this off there at any time. We never share any of this with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Your data is stored on your device

Memories — the titles, dates, notes, places, photos, and posters you create are stored on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). Markory never uploads them to a server, though places are looked up and shown using Apple Maps, as described below. Like your other app data, they're included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you use one, which is how they can be restored to a new iPhone.

Location — used only while you are using the app: to show the map around you, help you choose a place, sort memories by distance, or save the spot you're at. Markory never tracks your location in the background, and it stores your location only as the place of a memory you save.

Photos — Markory can only see the photos you choose, and keeps its own copy on your iPhone. When you choose a photo for a new memory, the place and date saved in the file are read on your iPhone to fill in any you haven't set yourself, and that place is sent to Apple Maps to find its name, as described below. Since version 1.1, the copy Markory keeps has that information removed. Photos added with version 1.0 were kept as you added them, so they can still contain it until you replace them in version 1.1 or later.

Reminders — if you turn on anniversary reminders, they are created on your iPhone, with no server of ours involved. Each one shows "On this day", a memory's title, how long ago it was, and, if other memories share that day, how many. Like your other notifications, they can appear wherever your notification settings allow, such as your Lock Screen, a paired Apple Watch, a Mac set up for iPhone Mirroring (even when your iPhone isn't nearby), or an accessory you've allowed to show notifications or read them aloud. You can turn them off entirely or for any single memory.

Widgets — while you have a Markory widget, a small copy of the title, place, and date of up to 30 memories is kept in a private folder that only Markory and its widget can read, along with poster images unless all your Markory widgets are on the Lock Screen. The folder is marked to be left out of device backups, and it's normally emptied the next time you open Markory after removing your last widget. Widgets show wherever you add them, such as your Home Screen, Lock Screen, Today View, StandBy, CarPlay, or your Mac. Everywhere except your Home Screen, they can be seen without unlocking your iPhone. Memories you've turned off on their own page are left out of widgets. Widgets on your iPhone normally update within seconds, and one on your Mac updates once your iPhone is near it or on the same Wi-Fi network.

Apple services

Maps, place search, and place names come from Apple Maps. What you type into place search, the area of the map you're viewing, a place you tap on the map to choose it, and any location being turned into a place name (for example when you use your current location or choose a photo that has a location) are sent to Apple to answer those requests, under Apple's privacy policy. If you've allowed Markory to use your location, Apple Maps may also receive it with these requests. Open in Maps hands a memory's location and place name to Apple's Maps app. We never receive any of this.

Sharing

Markory shares a poster or image only when you tap Share and choose where to send it. Shared images are newly drawn and carry no hidden location or camera details, but a poster shows whatever is on it. A memory's poster always shows a title, which you can change in the Poster Studio. By default it also shows the memory's photo, date, place (or its coordinates, if it has no name), and story if it has one, and you can turn each of these off there before you share. A Year in Memories poster always shows the year, how many memories and places it had, and how many memories fell in each month. By default it also shows up to six photos and the titles of the year's first and last named memories, and you can turn both off on the Year in Memories screen. Memories you've turned off on their own page still count in the totals, but their titles and photos are left off.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your memories stay on your iPhone until you delete them. A memory you delete is removed from Markory, and from its reminders and widgets the next time Markory updates them. Deleting Markory removes its data from your iPhone, including its widget folder and any reminders it scheduled. Posters you saved to Photos or shared stay where you put them, and a memory already in an iCloud or computer backup stays in that backup until the backup is replaced or deleted. You can turn off Markory's location access, notifications, or permission to add posters to Photos at any time in your iPhone's Settings. We hold none of your memories, so there's nothing for us to delete. We keep emails you send us in our Gmail account only as long as we need them to help you or fix what you reported, and you can ask us at any time to see or delete them.

Children

Markory is rated 4+, and the app collects no data from anyone, including children.
