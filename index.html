Common questions

Where are my memories stored?
Only on your iPhone. Markory has no account and no server — nothing is uploaded.

How do I delete a memory?
Swipe a memory in the list, or use the ⋯ menu → Select to remove several at once.

Does Markory track me?
No. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking. Location is used only while the app is open, to help you choose and browse places.
