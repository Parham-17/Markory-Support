Common questions

Where are my memories stored?
On your iPhone, and in its iCloud or computer backup if you use one. Reminders and widgets, if you use them, can also show some of a memory's details on other screens, such as an Apple Watch, a Mac or CarPlay. Markory has no account and no servers of ours, so your memories are never uploaded to us.

How do I delete a memory?
Swipe a memory in the list, or use the ⋯ menu → Select to remove several at once.

Does Markory track me?
No. Markory itself has no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking. If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Location is used only while the app is open, to show the map around you, help you choose a place, sort memories by distance, or save the spot you're at. Maps, place search and place names come from Apple Maps, which receives what you type into place search, the area of the map you're viewing, the places and locations being looked up, and possibly your location if you've allowed Markory to use it. None of it reaches us. The Privacy Policy has the details.
